# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and its users
- Showing empathy and kindness towards others

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Responsibilities and enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow them. Instances of unacceptable behaviour can be reported to the maintainer through GitHub. All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
